Project Overview

Engineering the Extraordinary:
"Make it Real"

Project MAIDEN (Multipurpose Autonomous Inorganic Divine Entity Network) was founded to engineer the next generation of companion, security, and relief operators — synthesizing cutting-edge robotics with human warmth.

The "Make it Real" Philosophy

For decades, anime and sci-fi imagined autonomous android maidens equipped with godlike psionic powers, sub-space weaponry, and flawless hospitality etiquette. At Project MAIDEN, our foundational principle is simple: do not keep them as mere concept sketches or fictional sprites. Make them real.

Every chassis designed in our development pipeline incorporates tangible physical packaging, rigorous mechanical engineering, verified CV work histories, and concrete combat parameter telemetry.

Operators Are Just Operators

We believe in clarity and honesty of purpose. While personas may carry evocative styling — whether the high-dimensional psionic resonance of a "Divine Goddess" or the hospitality-tuned precision of a "Droid Combat Maid" — these designations are thematic expressions of a single unified operator architecture.

Each operator (such as KIANA) exists as one integrated entity with switchable version matrices. You select the persona tailored to your mission requirements.

The Humanitarian Deployment Charter

While commercial clients and executive private details license operators at standard market rates, Project MAIDEN operates a permanent zero-cost grant program for certified humanitarian relief:

  • Disaster Search & Rescue: Operators equipped with Sub-Space Barrier Shields and hyper-sensory arrays deployed to earthquake and flood zones at 0 Credits.
  • Emergency Medical Transport: High-speed sub-orbital transit of critical vaccine and plasma supplies to isolated sectors.
  • Public Civic Protection: Stabilizing civic infrastructure and refugees with zero commercial fees.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does operator version switching work?

Operators like KIANA contain multi-layer firmware partitions. In the catalog, you can preview Divine Goddess and White Blossom versions on the same card, and select your preferred profile when opening the Deploy form.

What is the "I'm Feeling Lucky" button?

Inspired by classic search engine randomizers, clicking "I'm Feeling Lucky" generates an instantaneous deployment scenario — matching an operator persona with an unexpected mission sector and authorization profile.

How can administrators update the catalog and news?

Project MAIDEN is backed by a Git-based Static CMS (Sveltia/Decap CMS). Authorized staff can log into the /admin/ portal to publish new operators, modify CV work histories, or post press releases without manual code editing.
